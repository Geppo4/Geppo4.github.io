Privacy Policy — SoccerDisk Arena

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the SoccerDisk Arena Android app ("the App", "we") handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Developer / data controller: Geppo (SoccerDisk Arena).
Contact: privacygamegeppo@gmail.com

1. Information we handle

a) Game progress on your device. The App stores your progress on the device: coins, unlocked teams and items, match statistics, the list of purchases you made with their order numbers, and your settings (volumes, vibration, language). There is no account and we have no servers of our own: this data stays on your device.

b) Advertising (Google AdMob). The App shows ads through Google AdMob: rewarded videos, which you choose to watch, and full-screen ads between matches. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process your device's advertising ID, general device information, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interaction data and diagnostic data. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent management platform (UMP). In these countries you can change your choice at any time in the App: Settings → Privacy & ads → Manage.

c) In-app purchases. The App offers optional purchases (removal of full-screen ads, coins and packs with exclusive items). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play: we do not receive or store payment details. To deliver purchases, the App keeps the list of purchased products and the order numbers on your device, together with your game progress. For refunds and payment issues you can contact Google Play or write to us at the email above.

d) What we do NOT collect. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos or files. We have no servers of our own: data only goes through the Google services listed above. The App uses vibration, which you can turn off in the settings, and internet access, which is needed for ads and purchases.

2. How we use information

  • To save your game progress on your device.
  • To show ads, which keep the App free.
  • To deliver in-app purchases.
  • To make the App work properly and diagnose technical problems.

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Play services, Google Play Billing and Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from partner sites and apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Advertising choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you can change your consent in the App (Settings → Privacy & ads → Manage). Everywhere, on Android you can also delete or reset your advertising ID from Settings → Google → Ads. The Remove ads purchase turns off full-screen ads; rewarded videos stay optional.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your device: removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data in the system settings.
  • Data processed by Google (ads and payments): kept according to the Google policies linked above.
  • For any request you can email us at the address above.

6. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, please contact us.

7. Security

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Changes

We may update this Policy. Changes will be shown by updating the date at the top of the page.
