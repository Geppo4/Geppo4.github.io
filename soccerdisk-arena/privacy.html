Privacy Policy — SoccerDisk Arena

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This Policy explains how the SoccerDisk Arena application ("the App", "we") handles information.

Developer / data controller: Geppo (SoccerDisk Arena).
Contact: privacygamegeppo@gmail.com

1. Data collected

None. The App runs entirely on your device: it requires no sign-up, collects no personal data and sends nothing to us or to third parties. It contains no ads, in-app purchases, usage analytics or third-party services, and it does not request permission to access the internet.

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores your game progress on your device: coins, unlocked teams and items, match statistics and settings (volumes, vibration, language). This data stays on your device and is never transmitted. It is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data in the system settings.

3. Permissions

The App only uses vibration, for haptic feedback while playing; you can turn it off in the App's settings. It does not access location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

4. Children

The App collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

5. Changes

If the App changes (for example by adding ads or online services), this Policy will be updated before that update is published, with the new date shown at the top of the page.
